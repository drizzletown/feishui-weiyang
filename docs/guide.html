READER'S GUIDE / 阅读指南

The Fei River, Unending 淝水未央

Six volumes · 75 chapters. A Chinese-language alternate-history novel imagining a different outcome of the fateful encounter at the Fei River.

Historical names / 历史人物

Fu Jian · 苻坚
Heavenly King of Former Qin in the historical setting; a ruler whose decision changes the novel’s course
Wang Meng · 王猛
statesman and strategist whose deathbed counsel begins the story
Fu Rong · 苻融
Fu Jian’s brother and a senior military commander
Fu Hong · 苻宏
the crown prince, associated with the reform of state institutions
Murong Chui · 慕容垂
a former Yan prince and commander serving Qin
Yao Chang · 姚苌
a Qiang leader whose loyalties are tested
Zhu Xu · 朱序
a Jin commander whose allegiance remains central to the plot
Xie An · 谢安
Eastern Jin statesman
Xie Xuan · 谢玄
Eastern Jin military leader
Liu Yu · 刘裕
rising Jin commander

What is historical—and what is imagined?

The book uses historical people and dynastic titles but deliberately diverges from the received historical events of the Battle of the Fei River. Events after that divergence are the novel’s invention and should not be read as factual history.

Reading / 阅读方式

全书六卷七十五章，可选择“中文”“EN”或“对照”模式阅读。字号和明暗主题可在页面顶部调整；每章末尾可前往上一章或下一章。

All seventy-five chapters can be read in Chinese, English, or a paragraph-aligned bilingual view. Use the controls at the top to adjust type size and color theme.
